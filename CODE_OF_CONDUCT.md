# Academic & Research Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As researchers, students, faculty, and open-source contributors associated with the **Molecule-AI** project, we pledge to foster a professional, respectful, and dignified research environment. We are committed to conducting scientific inquiry with honesty, integrity, and mutual respect for all collaborators and contributors.

---

## Professional & Scientific Standards

We encourage and expect behaviors that foster rigorous scholarship and positive academic collaboration, including:

* **Professional Courtesy**: Treating all collaborators, students, and researchers with dignity, kindness, and respect.
* **Scientific Integrity**: Upholding rigorous standards in data collection, benchmarking, experimental reproducibility, and model evaluation.
* **Constructive Dialogue**: Engaging in thoughtful, evidence-based scientific discourse and receiving technical feedback gracefully.
* **Transparency & Accountability**: Acknowledging errors promptly, giving proper attribution to previous works, and adhering to strict academic citation standards.
* **Collaborative Spirit**: Supporting fellow researchers and students in advancing computational chemistry and machine learning research.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

To preserve a professional and constructive academic environment, the following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

* **Personal Attacks & Derogatory Conduct**: Insulting, demeaningly dismissive, or abusive remarks directed toward any contributor.
* **Harassment & Intimidation**: Public or private harassment, threats, or hostile communication in any form.
* **Academic Dishonesty**: Falsification or fabrication of experimental results, intentional benchmark distortion, or plagiarism.
* **Unauthorized Disclosure**: Publishing private communication or proprietary academic data without explicit permission.
* **Disruptive Conduct**: Any behavior that compromises the collaborative focus, academic reputation, or integrity of the research group.

---

## Scope & Responsibility

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issue trackers, discussions, pull requests, and any academic forums where contributors officially represent the **Molecule-AI** research initiative.

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding these standards and have the authority to remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, or pull requests that violate professional ethics.
